Designing a Global Video Upload and Playback System at Scale

An in-depth conversation between two engineers collaboratively designing a large-scale video platform that supports millions of creators and billions of views globally with adaptive streaming, secure storage, and cost-effective delivery using cloud infrastructure.

Design challenge: Design a system supporting video upload and playback at large scale, handling billions of daily views and millions of creators across multiple client types worldwide, balancing availability, latency, and storage.
